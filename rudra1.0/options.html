👔 Rudra 1.0 — Settings

When off, Rudra 1.0 won't scan pages or intercept consent buttons anywhere.
Cookie/consent buttons are always paused for review first — the extension never silently blocks you, it just shows what it found before you click "Accept" for real.

All scanning happens locally in your browser using a fixed checklist of common risky clauses (data selling, arbitration, auto-renewal, broad content licenses, etc). Nothing is sent to any server.
