Install Your Custom Hackathon Project (Developer Mode)

1. Open Google Chrome and type chrome://extensions/ into the URL address bar.
2. Toggle the Developer mode switch to the "ON" position in the top right corner.
3. Click the Load unpacked button that appears in the top left menu.
4. Select the exact folder on your computer that contains your project's code files.
5. Navigate to any website of your choice in a new tab.
6. Scroll to the bottom and click on its Privacy Policy page to test your extension's scanning capabilities.
